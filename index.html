Twisted with love

Wonder Loops is a creative school Skill Fest project featuring handmade pipe-cleaner flowers.

It begins with a bundle of simple, fuzzy pipe cleaners. With a little bending, twisting and patience, they become tulips, sunflowers, lavender and lilies in soft, happy colors.

Every flower is shaped by hand, petal by petal, and each one takes care and a lot of small decisions. We love that creativity can turn something ordinary into something you want to keep.

We made these flowers together, and we are excited to share them with you at our stall.
